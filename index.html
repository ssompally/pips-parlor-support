Support and help

Getting help

Something broken, a level that will not finish, or a purchase that did not arrive? Email ssompally@gmail.com and we will get back to you. Please include your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. If you would rather report it in public you can also open an issue, which needs a GitHub account; email does not.

Common questions

My coins are missing. Coins are stored on the device, not on an account, so they do not move to a new phone and they do not survive deleting the app. If a purchase was charged but no coins arrived, email us with the date and the amount.

Can I restore a coin purchase? Coin packs are consumable — they are spent on the helpers and life refills you buy with them, so there is nothing to restore. Restoring is still available in the Counter and will tell you the same thing.

Do I have to pay? No. Coins are earned by serving orders, orders without a helper pay a tip, and lives refill on their own over time. Coin packs only save waiting.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 September 2026.

Pip's Parlor does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or location.

What stays on your device. Your progress, coin balance, helper counts, lives, and sound settings are saved on the device only. The game also keeps a local file of gameplay events (which level started, finished, or was retried) so you can send it to us yourself from Settings if you are reporting a problem. Nothing in that file is sent anywhere unless you choose to share it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases. Coin packs are sold by Apple. We never see your payment details. Purchases are managed for us by RevenueCat, which receives a randomly generated identifier for your installation, your device model and operating system version, your App Store country, and the purchase receipt Apple issues. That identifier is not linked to your name or to any account, and it is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What we do not do. There is no advertising in this app, no advertising identifier, no analytics service, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites.

Children. The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions about privacy, including any request about data held about you, can be sent to ssompally@gmail.com.

Pip's Parlor is made by an independent developer.
